## Overview

*Make It Here* brought together Oregon Film, North East Productions, and Cine Rent West on a single short film. Oregon Film is the state's official film office and support body for the Oregon film industry. I developed and led the project as producer, DP, and co-editor, from the pitch to the final cut.

![Behind the scenes on Make It Here, collaborating at the camera](/images/editorial/make-it-here-01.jpg)

## Role

I handled the full arc of production: conceiving and pitching the project, leading the camera department on set, and co-editing the final cut.

![On-set interview setup for Make It Here, crew and green screen behind](/images/editorial/make-it-here-02.jpg)

## Social Cutdowns

Once the film was locked, I cut it down for social: nine vertical edits, each built to work on its own. Most are single interview moments with the people who make film happen in Oregon, alongside behind-the-scenes and location b-roll and the poster drop. They rolled out post by post on Cine Rent West's Instagram across the season, each one pointing back to the full film.

One interview cut broke out on its own and passed 144,000 views. Across all nine, the campaign brought in about 187,000 views.
