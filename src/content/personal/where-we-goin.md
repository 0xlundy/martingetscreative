## The Film

*Where We Goin?: The Power of Place* is a 29-minute documentary from North East Productions, directed and narrated by Devin Boss. Devin carries one question through Portland, often called one of the whitest cities in America: where are we going? He traces his own roots and the Black artists, cultural stewards and community leaders who have shaped the city, then and now.

The film follows artist Intisar Abioto through her curation at the Portland Art Museum and her work to reclaim the legacy of civil rights pioneer Beatrice Morrow Cannady. It walks the old Albina neighborhood with Albina Vision Trust, whose restorative redevelopment is one of the largest efforts of its kind in the country, and sits down with longtime Albina leader Sharon Gary-Smith.

It started as a short. Episode One, *DJ O.G. One x The Creative Homies*, followed Portland DJ O.G. One back to the record store and the Alberta Street block he grew up on. It won Best Documentary Short at Portland Film Festival in 2023, and the project grew into *The Power of Place*.

![Where We Goin, Episode One title card: DJ O.G. One x The Creative Homies, with Portland Film Festival and McMinnville Short Film Festival laurels](/images/personal/where-we-goin/wwg-episode-one-poster.jpg)

## Premiere and Broadcast

The film premiered to a packed house and a standing ovation at the Hollywood Theatre in November 2024. It made its broadcast debut on OPB on November 9, 2026. It's streaming on OPB now, and nationwide streaming on the PBS app is coming soon.

## My Role

I was Director of Photography on both shoots, building the look with Devin: natural light, real places around Portland, and room for the conversations to breathe.
